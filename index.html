FAQ

Quick answers about how Sentrik LLC relates to the brands you work with.

What is Sentrik LLC?

Sentrik LLC is the parent company that owns and supports our operating businesses. It provides governance, shared resources, and long-term strategy while each brand serves customers in its own market.

Who do I contact for a specific service?

Reach out to the brand that matches your project: NTX Lighting, Sentrik Roofing, Sentrik Rentals, or Sentrik Security. Use the contact section on this page for general inquiries sent to Sentrik LLC.

Are all brands part of the same company?

They share ownership under Sentrik LLC but operate as distinct businesses with dedicated teams. That keeps accountability clear and expertise deep in each trade.

Where are you located?

Operations are rooted in North Texas. Exact service areas and dispatch details vary by brand; contact the relevant business for coverage and scheduling.
